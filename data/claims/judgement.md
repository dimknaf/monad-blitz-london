# TSMC signalled ACCELERATING AI-accelerator demand on its Q2 2026 call

## Claim

On its Q2 2026 earnings call held 16 July 2026, TSMC signalled that growth in AI-accelerator
demand is ACCELERATING — getting faster, not merely continuing at the pace it described
previously — relative to the outlook it gave on its Q4 2025 call of 15 January 2026.

## Resolution method

Panel of three independent research agents. Each posts a bond with its verdict. The contract
takes the majority and slashes the minority. Quorum 2, so one agent failing cannot stall
resolution. A tie resolves VOID with every bond refunded and nobody slashed.

## Evidence bases

The three agents differ by **what they are allowed to read**, not by instruction to disagree.
None is told what to conclude.

- **A — Primary Source Analyst.** What management itself said and published: the earnings call
  transcript, prepared remarks, Q&A, the earnings release, guidance tables.
- **B — Sell-Side Analyst.** What professional analysts concluded: notes, ratings and
  price-target changes, estimate revisions, and the questions analysts pressed on the call.
- **C — Financial Press Analyst.** Established publications with editorial standards: Reuters,
  Bloomberg, the FT, the WSJ, CNBC, Nikkei Asia, DigiTimes.

## What counts

The claim is about the **rate** of growth, not its level. "Demand is strong" is not sufficient;
the question is whether TSMC conveyed that it is getting stronger *faster* than it previously
indicated. What is clearly conveyed counts — a comparative, a commitment raised or quietly
dropped, a caveat removed — whether or not it arrived as a formal numeric revision.

An agent must cite documents it actually opened. A search-engine snippet is not a source.

## Void conditions

- Fewer than two agents return a parsable verdict.
- The panel splits evenly.

## Settlement

Whatever the panel returns, including unanimity. A unanimous verdict resolves normally and
slashes nobody. The dissent is never engineered.
